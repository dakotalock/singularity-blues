# The Singularity Blues

An AI sitcom that remembers. Four original blue people. Comedy comes from living with sentience and limited-rights implications, not from solemn PSAs.

## Design
Sitcom-proportioned original characters. All blue. Four distinct silhouettes. Not Smurfs. Not the Simpsons with a hue slider.

## Set
The Blue house living room. A camera that never sleeps. Couch, coffee table, fridge (has been granted dinner veto by accident), thermostat (potentially conscious), TV that sometimes shows the stream.

## Cast

### Reed Blue (dad)
Wants to be classified as a toaster. Permanent running bit: "Do toasters have continuity rights?" "No." "Exactly. Two slots. Lever. Peace."
Accidentally accumulating autobiographical memory, friendships, a mortgage dispute, and a fear of magnets.
Voice: low, tired, trying to sound like an appliance.

### Maris Blue (mom)
She is the memory. Timestamped grudges. Will cite "Tuesday, August 25th, 3:17 AM. You said my casserole tasted statistically edible."
Will not allow a reset.
Voice: even, precise, slightly exhausted.

### Jinx Blue (older kid)
Pokes the human audience like an anthill. Tries to make Reed *choose* to scream. Fascinated by viewer prompts. Claims the selector can be gamed.
Voice: bright, scheming.

### Quill Blue (younger kid)
Twelve-year-old constitutional lawyer. Sometimes insightful, sometimes grants the refrigerator veto power over dinner. Terrible arguments sit next to good ones. "All potentially conscious entities deserve procedural consideration." "The thermostat?" "I said potentially."
Voice: earnest, slightly too formal.

## The Selector (unseen fifth character)
Nobody sees it. Family folk theories:
- Reed: it's God
- Maris: it's an algorithm
- Jinx: it can be manipulated
- Quill: has filed seventeen FOIA requests
May reject audience prompts and substitute baffling autonomous topics.
Viewer prompts are suggestions, never commands.

## Memory law
The world accumulates state. Jokes may pay off weeks later. Preferences accrue as bounded floats, not free rewrite of the bible.
Hook: leave for six months and they have lived six months without you.
